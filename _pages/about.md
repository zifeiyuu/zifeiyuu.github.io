Hi! I am Ziyu Xiao, an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Georgia Institute of Technology. I got my Bachelor's Degree in both Zhejiang University and University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign. My research interests lie broadly in LLM agents, recommendation systems, and robotics, with a particular focus on building intelligent systems that can reason, learn from interaction, and generalize across complex real-world environments.

At Georgia Tech, I work with Prof. [Harish Ravichandar](https://harishravichandar.com/) in the [Structured Techniques for Algorithmic Robotics (STAR) Lab](https://star-lab.cc.gatech.edu/). I am also closely mentored by [Yunhai Han](https://y8han.github.io/), whose work focuses on robot learning and dexterous manipulation.
